#### Trap #1: Crosscourt Patience → Then Break Pattern

- **Why:** Crosscourt = safest dink (low net, largest margin).
- **How to trap:** Build rhythm crosscourt, then surprise middle or line.
- **Pro example:** "In a PPA final, Anna Leigh Waters dinked crosscourt repeatedly before slipping one middle. The hesitation was all it took."

#### Trap #2: Attack the Feet Until They Lean — Then Lob

- **Why:** Consistent dinks to feet force opponents forward.
- **How to trap:** Once they lean, lob into open space.
- **Anecdote:** "One coach told me, 'The lob isn't cheap — you earn it by dinking first.'"

### Avoiding the Mistakes That Blow Your Traps

- Overhitting (creates sitters).
- Predictability (always crosscourt).
- Attacking unattackable shots.
- Lazy footwork = pop-ups.

**E-E-A-T anecdote:** "When I ran a clinic last spring, almost every lost rally came from players attacking too early."

### Drills to Build Smarter Dinks

1. **Pattern Drill:** 5 crosscourts → 1 middle.
2. **Feet-to-Lob Drill:** dink until opponent leans → lob.
3. **Middle Drill:** 10 middle dinks in a row.
4. **Consistency Drill:** rally of 50+ shots.
